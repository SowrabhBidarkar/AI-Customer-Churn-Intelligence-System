# AI-Customer-Churn-Intelligence-System
## Customer Churn Prediction, Explainable AI & Business Intelligence Dashboard

An end-to-end customer churn analytics project that combines SQL, Python, Machine Learning, SHAP Explainability, and Power BI to identify customers at risk of churn, understand the key factors influencing churn, and support data-driven customer retention decisions.
## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is a major business problem for subscription-based and telecom companies. Losing customers can directly impact recurring revenue and increase the cost of acquiring new customers.

The objective of this project is to build an analytics solution that answers four important business questions:

What is happening?
How many customers are churning and how much revenue is associated with them?
Who is leaving?
Which customer segments, contracts, services, and payment methods show higher churn?
Why are customers leaving?
Which customer attributes are the most important drivers of churn according to the Machine Learning model?
Which customers are at higher risk?
Can the model identify customers who are more likely to churn so that business teams can prioritize them?
